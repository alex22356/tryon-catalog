Fitting Room

Put the outfit together
before you go shopping

A mobile app for shoppers in Ireland. Build a look on a virtual mannequin, compare what it costs across high-street stores, then head out and buy it.

Android app — in development

How it works

01

Set your budget

Move the slider and the catalogue shows only what fits — from €5 basics upwards.

02

Dress the mannequin

Pick a top, bottom and shoes. They appear on the mannequin instantly, so you can see the whole look together.

03

See where to buy

Every item lists its store and price, with the total for the outfit and a link through to the retailer.

Inside the app

Stores we cover

  • Dunnes Stores
  • Penneys
  • Lidl
  • Primark
  • M&S Ireland

Prices shown in the app are refreshed daily and each item is stamped with the date it was last checked. Where a price is indicative rather than confirmed, the app marks it clearly so shoppers know to verify in store.

What we are

Fitting Room is a price comparison and inspiration tool. We do not sell anything ourselves — we help people plan an outfit within a budget and point them to the retailer selling it.
