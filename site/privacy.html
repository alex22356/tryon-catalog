Privacy Policy

Fitting Room (Android app) · Last updated: 26 July 2026

Fitting Room helps you put an outfit together and see where to buy it. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data. In short: your photo stays on your phone unless you explicitly ask for an AI feature, and we do not run accounts, ads or analytics.

What the app does on your device only

Choosing a photo, detecting your body, cutting out garments and dressing the mannequin all happen entirely offline on your phone. That photo is never uploaded and we never see it.

When your photo leaves the device

Only if you tap one of the optional AI features, and only after you agree to it in the app:

  • Realistic try-on — your photo and the chosen garment images are sent to Google's Gemini image model to generate a realistic result. Google processes them under its own terms; see the Gemini API terms.
  • Stylist advice — your photo is sent to an AI model to comment on the outfit. This feature only works if an AI endpoint is configured; in the public release it is disabled by default.

We do not store your photos on any server of ours. We have no server that receives them — requests go directly from your phone to the AI provider.

What we collect

  • No account is required. You can use the app as a guest.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or device identifiers.

Network requests the app makes

  • The product catalogue is downloaded from GitHub Pages (catalog.json) and cached on your device.
  • Product images are loaded directly from the retailers' own image servers.
  • Those services can see your IP address, as with any website you visit.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights (GDPR)

We hold no personal data about you, so there is normally nothing to export or erase on our side. Photos and saved settings live on your phone — clearing the app's data removes them. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will deal with it within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
